## Welcome to Spark Foundry

Spark Foundry is an AI-powered workspace built on the Contentstack DXP ecosystem. It helps teams collect information from many sources — links, images, documents, Slack threads, Google Drive files, CMS entries — and use AI to synthesize that information into business-ready artifacts like CMS entries and campaign briefs.

### Key Capabilities

- **Multi-source collection** — Save links, images, notes, files, Slack threads, Google Drive documents, Contentstack entries, and Microsoft Clarity analytics data into a single workspace
- **AI-powered chat** — Ask questions about your collected data using semantic search (RAG) with Claude as the analyst
- **Artifact generation** — Transform raw research into CMS entries, campaign briefs, and custom outputs
- **Collaborative editing** — Real-time document editing with TipTap and Y.js collaboration
- **Visual canvas** — Drag-and-drop spatial canvas for grouping and organizing items
- **Vector visualization** — See relationships between your data as a 2D/3D vector space
