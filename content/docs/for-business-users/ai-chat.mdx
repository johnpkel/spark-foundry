## AI Chat

The AI chat in Spark Foundry isn't a generic chatbot — it's a **Spark analyst** that has read and understood everything in your workspace. It's designed to be direct, evidence-based, and sometimes opinionated.

### How It Works

When you send a message, the system:

1. **Searches your data** — Finds the most relevant items using semantic similarity (not just keyword matching)
2. **Includes images** — If relevant items have images, the AI can see and reason about them
3. **Recalls past conversations** — Previous chat sessions in the same Spark are searchable too
4. **Responds with evidence** — Cites specific items by name in a Sources section

### Effective Prompting

**Be specific about what you want:**
- Instead of "summarize this," try "summarize the competitive positioning themes across these three articles"
- Instead of "write a blog post," try "draft a 500-word blog post targeting enterprise CMOs about headless CMS migration, using the research I've collected"

**Ask analytical questions:**
- "What contradictions exist between these data sources?"
- "Which of these campaign angles has the strongest supporting evidence?"
- "What's missing from my research that I should look into?"

**Request specific formats:**
- "Create a comparison table of the three competitors"
- "Give me 5 bullet points I can use in a presentation"
- "Draft this as a Contentstack CMS entry with SEO fields"

### Using @ Mentions

Type `@` in the chat to reference specific items by name. This tells the AI to pay special attention to those items:

- "@Product Launch Deck — what are the key dates mentioned?"
- "Compare @Competitor A Analysis with @Competitor B Analysis"
- "Use @Q1 Revenue Report to draft talking points"

### Canvas-Scoped Chat

When you select items on the visual canvas and then chat, the AI focuses exclusively on those items. This is powerful for:

- Analyzing a specific subset of your research
- Comparing items you've grouped together
- Generating content from a curated selection

### Editor Integration

If you have text selected in the document editor when you chat, the AI can:
- Suggest rewrites and improvements
- Expand or condense the selected text
- Adjust tone (more formal, more casual, etc.)
- Add data-backed claims from your Spark items

Suggestions appear as "proposal" blocks that you can accept or reject.

### Web Research

The AI can search the web on your behalf:
- "Research the latest trends in headless CMS adoption"
- "Find case studies of companies migrating from monolithic to headless CMS"

After researching, findings are saved back to your Spark for future reference. This means the research compounds over time.

### Chat Sessions

Each conversation is saved as a session. You can:
- View past sessions in the chat panel dropdown
- Continue previous conversations
- Start fresh sessions for new topics

Sessions are also semantically indexed, so if you previously discussed a topic in an earlier session, the AI will find and reference that context.

### Reading the AI's Work

During complex requests, you'll see indicators of what the AI is doing:

- **Searching knowledge base...** — Running semantic search on your items
- **Tool calls** — The AI is using a specific tool (search, web scrape, etc.)
- **Thinking** — Internal reasoning visible as a collapsible thought process
- **Approval requests** — Write operations (like creating CMS entries) require your approval before executing

### Tips

- The AI is designed to push back on weak ideas. If it says an approach has problems, consider the feedback seriously.
- End your messages with a clear ask: "...so should I pursue this angle?" gets a better response than open-ended prompts.
- Use the activity log (lightning bolt icon) to see exactly what API calls the AI is making — useful for understanding how it found certain information.
