## Use Cases

Spark Foundry is flexible enough to support many workflows. Here are some proven patterns.

### Content Strategy & Calendar Planning

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark for your content calendar period (e.g., "Q2 Content Strategy")
2. Import existing CMS entries from Contentstack to understand current content
3. Add links to competitor content and industry publications
4. Import Clarity analytics to see which existing pages perform best
5. Ask the AI: "Based on our existing content performance and competitive gaps, what topics should we prioritize?"
6. Generate CMS entry drafts for the top recommendations

**Why it works:** The AI can cross-reference your analytics data (what performs), your existing content (what you've covered), and competitive content (what gaps exist) to make data-backed recommendations.

### Competitive Intelligence

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark per competitor or a single "Competitive Landscape" Spark
2. Add links to competitor websites, pricing pages, feature pages
3. Save screenshots of competitor UIs as images (the AI can analyze them)
4. Import relevant Slack threads where the team discussed competitors
5. Have the AI research recent news and industry reports
6. Ask: "Create a competitive comparison matrix" or "Where are we differentiated vs. [competitor]?"

**Why it works:** The combination of web scraping, image analysis (for screenshots), and team context (Slack) gives the AI a holistic competitive view that goes beyond surface-level comparisons.

### Campaign Brief Development

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark for the campaign (e.g., "Product Launch — Headless CMS")
2. Collect target audience research, market data, and brand guidelines
3. Add successful past campaign examples from Drive or CMS
4. Import relevant customer feedback or Slack discussions
5. Ask the AI to generate a campaign brief with specific sections
6. Iterate: "Sharpen the target audience section" or "Add KPIs based on our Clarity data"

**Why it works:** Campaign briefs benefit from structured synthesis of many inputs. The AI can draw from quantitative (analytics), qualitative (customer feedback), and strategic (brand guidelines) sources simultaneously.

### Customer Research Synthesis

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark for the research initiative
2. Import customer interview transcripts or notes from Drive
3. Add survey results and NPS feedback
4. Import relevant Slack threads from customer-facing channels
5. Add Clarity behavioral data showing user friction points
6. Ask: "What are the top 5 customer pain points with supporting evidence?"

**Why it works:** Customer insights are often scattered across surveys, support tickets, Slack conversations, and analytics. Spark Foundry brings them together and lets the AI find patterns across all sources.

### Editorial Collaboration

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark for the piece (e.g., "Annual Report — 2025 Trends")
2. Collect source material: industry reports, internal data, expert quotes
3. Use the rich text editor to draft the content
4. Highlight sections and ask the AI for feedback or expansion
5. Use @ mentions to tie specific claims to supporting research
6. Generate the final CMS entry for publication

**Why it works:** The editor + chat + research combination means you can write, research, and refine without switching tools. Highlighting text and asking for improvements keeps the AI focused on exactly what you need.

### Meeting Prep & Briefing Docs

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark for the meeting topic
2. Import relevant Drive documents (agendas, previous meeting notes)
3. Add any research links that inform the discussion
4. Save relevant Slack threads for context
5. Ask: "Create a one-page briefing doc for this meeting, including key discussion points and open questions"

**Why it works:** Meeting prep often requires pulling context from multiple sources. Spark Foundry consolidates everything and can generate a structured briefing in seconds.

### Content Migration Planning

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark for the migration project
2. Import all entries from the source Contentstack stack
3. Add the target content model documentation
4. Ask the AI to analyze the content and recommend a migration mapping
5. Generate sample migrated entries to validate the approach

**Why it works:** The AI can understand content type structures, field mappings, and content patterns at scale — work that would take a human days to audit.

### Brand Voice Development

**The workflow:**
1. Create a Spark for "Brand Voice"
2. Import 10-20 of your best-performing content pieces from the CMS
3. Add competitor content for contrast
4. Add brand guidelines from Drive
5. Ask: "Analyze the writing patterns in our top content. What defines our brand voice?"
6. Generate a brand voice guide based on the analysis

**Why it works:** Brand voice is best defined by example, not prescription. The AI can identify patterns across many pieces that a human might not notice.
