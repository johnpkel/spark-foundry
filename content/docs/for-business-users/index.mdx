## Welcome to Spark Foundry

Spark Foundry is your AI-powered workspace for turning scattered research into structured business output. Think of it as a smart research assistant that remembers everything you feed it and helps you make sense of it all.

### What Can You Do?

- **Collect** — Save links, images, notes, Slack threads, Google Drive docs, CMS entries, and analytics data into a single workspace
- **Organize** — Use the visual canvas to group related items, or let the AI find connections you might have missed
- **Ask** — Chat with an AI analyst that has read and understood everything in your workspace
- **Create** — Generate CMS entries, campaign briefs, and other business artifacts from your collected research
